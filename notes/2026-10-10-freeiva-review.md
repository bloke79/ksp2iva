# FreeIva review: what to bring to IVA Redux (2026-10-10)

Sources: FreeIva 0.2.20.2 as installed in KSP1 (`GameData/FreeIva`, configs read), its source on GitHub
(FirstPersonKSP/FreeIva, read for ideas only), stock KSP1 IVA configs (`GameData/Squad/Spaces`), and the KSP2 stock
interior prefabs.

## How FreeIva works (the parts worth porting)
| Mechanic | How FreeIva does it | KSP2 Redux version |
|---|---|---|
| Unbuckle and move (Y, then WASD, Shift/Ctrl up/down, Q/E roll, Space jump) | Invisible capsule with a rigidbody, origin at the eye. A separate camera anchor turns with the mouse. Movement adds velocity changes capped by an acceleration limit. In gravity it walks with ground detection and a walkable slope; weightless, it drifts with damping. | A kinematic capsule in **pod-local space**, moved by our own capsule sweeps against the interior's collision layer, so nothing touches the game's physics. Gravity comes from the vessel's acceleration (API to verify). Roll and drift when weightless. |
| Collision shell | Each interior names a `shellCollider` mesh, plus box colliders for props. | The exporter writes `IVA_Collider_*` objects (a simplified hull, plus boxes for consoles and couches). Set Up Interiors turns them into colliders on one layer, with no renderer. |
| Buckle back in | Look at a seat or click the harness buckle prop. A seat-belt sound plays and the view returns to the seat. | Look at a couch and press F, or Y to return to your own seat. We hide the game's seated kerbal for your seat while you're out. |
| Hatches to other parts | Hatch props are tied to attach nodes. Openings are cut into shells at load time. Connected parts' interiors load and you can pass through. Blocked hatches show a closed door. | Interiors declare their hatches in the spec (node and position). At run time, interiors for connected crew parts load and the hatch opens when both sides have one. This needs crew-cabin and tunnel interiors. |
| Window views | `InternalCameraSwitch` colliders: click a window to glide the camera there with a narrower field of view. Click again to go back. The cupola has six. | `IVA_View_*` markers in each model. Click a window, or press a key to cycle views. **Cheap, high value.** |
| Hiding the outside from inside | Depth-mask hulls: an invisible mesh writes depth first, so the part's exterior and other parts don't draw inside the cabin. | Fixes the "pod exterior visible inside" problem properly, replacing the current hide-exterior toggle. Needs a depth-only shader in our bundle. |
| Weightless aids | Hand-rail colliders you can grab. Ladder state for climbing. | Later, once movement exists. |
| Physical props | Grabbable, throwable props (for example the fire extinguisher). | Fun later. |

## Design ideas from the stock KSP1 IVAs
- **Live instruments.** The navball turns, and the altimeter (three hands), vertical speed, radar altitude,
  atmosphere depth, pitch/yaw/roll needles, speed LEDs and throttle lever all move. Ours are static textures. Name the
  moving parts in Blender (`IVA_Needle_alt`, `IVA_Navball`, …) and let the mod drive them from flight data. **This is
  the biggest visual upgrade available.**
- **Clickable switches.** KSP1's guarded switches and cluster switches toggle SAS, RCS, gear, lights and stage. A
  raycast from the mouse plus a collider per switch gives the same, with a flip animation.
- **Window views per seat.** The cupola has a view for each window. Our Mk1-3 rendezvous and side windows suit this.
- **Stowage.** Lander cans have cargo bags. Our lockers and nets fit, and could later link to inventory.

## KSP2 interiors: what exists, and the pose rule
The catalogue has only these crewed interior prefabs: `interior_1v_m1_pod_crew`, `interior_2v_m1_pod_crew`, the
cockpits `interior_1v_m1_cockpit_crew(_inline)`, `interior_2v_m2_cockpit_crew(_inline)` and `interior_3v_m3_cockpit_crew`,
and `interior_seat_command` / `interior_0v_seat_external`. **Every kerbal faces the part's +Y (nose) axis with their head
towards -Z.** In a vertical pod that means lying on their back. In a plane cockpit it means sitting upright and facing
forward. Cockpit eye points, from the prefabs in `C:\Games\KSP2Mods\reference\poses`:

| Cockpit | Seats | Eye (part space) |
|---|---|---|
| Mk1 cockpit | 1 | (0, 0.463, -0.182) |
| Mk1 inline | 1 | (0, 0.101, -0.489) |
| Mk2 cockpit | 2 | ±0.32 apart (prefab has an offset root; take positions from the in-game capture) |
| Mk3 cockpit | 4 | front pair x ±0.488 at y 0.302; rear pair x ±0.418 at y -0.575; all z -0.987 |

Landers, the cupola, and the 1.5v and 3v pods have no prefab of their own, so they reuse one of these. **Entering IVA
once in each** makes the pose dump record which one, with the real poses. Then `pose_extract.py` produces the spec and
`capsule.py` builds the interior, as for the Mk1 and Mk1-3.

## Proposed roadmap
1. **Pose capture sweep** (Matt, about 10 minutes): enter IVA once in each crewed part: 1.5v and 3v pods, the three
   landers, cupola, and the Mk1/Mk2/Mk3 cockpits.
2. **Window views**: `IVA_View_*` markers plus a click or key to glide there. Small; works in both existing interiors.
3. **Live instruments**: navball, needles, lights and the event timer driven by flight data. Medium. Needs the moving
   parts split out in the generator.
4. **Next interiors** from the capture: cupola (window views shine here), landers (looking down at the ground),
   1.5v and 3v pods, then cockpits (upright, dashboard ahead, canopy).
5. **Unbuckle and move** inside one interior: collision export, kinematic capsule, buckle back. Large.
6. **Hatches between parts**: crew cabins, tunnels and docking. Largest; depends on 5 and on cabin interiors.
7. **Clickable switches**, and **depth masks** for a clean exterior.
